# wohl v0.8.0 — release plan

Status: draft, owner approval pending (queued decision). Nothing here is a
production claim; every item is work in progress until the tag's own gates
say otherwise. Planned 2026-11-19 against the PulseEngine north star;
adversarially checked by a cold second pass, and revised from its findings.

## Theme

v0.7.0 proved the evidence bundle exists; v0.8.0 makes it impossible to
ship without it and makes what it attests true. The bundle becomes a
release invariant checkable by someone outside the project; the headline
safety finding from the requirements review closes all the way down; the
gap between claimed and proven becomes a machine-checked ledger that may
only shrink; and the door path produces its first signed
microcontroller image, honestly labeled as emulated.

## Bets

### 1. evidence-everywhere (SWREQ-REL-002) — objective O2
The bundle goes from once-achieved to invariant: a tag cannot publish
with a failing bundle, the negative controls become per-piece (trace,
MC/DC report, attestation, SHA256SUMS, cosign bundle - each proven red
in CI), verify instructions are generated from the publishing run so the
cosign identity always matches the publish path, and one recorded
offline run by a non-maintainer closes the outsider-checkable claim.
Kill: an outsider needs an undocumented step, network access or a
locally built tool; or a per-piece control stays green with its piece
removed. Delta over v0.7.0 is hardening, not creation.

### 2. zero-open-safety-findings — owner priority (b); no objective's indicator, honestly
Close the requirements review to the bottom: the TEMP-003 window
detector merged with its Kani harnesses (in progress), SWREQ-POWER
authored (#96), the alert WIT regenerated from AADL via spar to match
the verified engine (#99), and every deferred #97 item either pinned
with a boundary test or answered. A re-filed item does NOT count as
closed: re-filing is explicitly scope removal, listed in the release
notes as open. Kill: rivet going green by deleting a finding rather
than answering it.

### 3. trust-escape-ledger (SWREQ-REL-003) — objective O3, conditionally O5
The O3 indicator made real: a required check enumerating every
input-narrowing assumption (kani assumes, harness preconditions, Verus
axioms, proptest narrowing) across all seven engines and the Verus
proofs, failing on growth against a committed baseline; the ledger ships
in the evidence bundle. The tool is Rust; it counts toward O5 only once
it runs as a signed WASI component under kiln with an in-CI equivalence
test - otherwise no O5 claim. Kill: unsound enumeration or one observed
baseline-bump-to-green without a proof change demotes it to a report.

### 4. door-on-emulated-target (SWREQ-REL-004) — O1 preparation, feeds O2
A renode lane boots the synth-built armv6-m door image, injects a
contact event at the gale seam and asserts the alert; the signed image
ships as a release asset. This does NOT move O1's indicator - an
emulated run is, by O1's own guard, a skipped hardware run - and the
plan says so rather than filing emulator evidence as more than it is.
Kill (hard date): synth's armv6-m lane plus its dissolve fix not merged
three weeks before the intended tag kills the item for this release.

### Cut: relay-sec on the bench link (#49)
Cut from committed scope on the checker's finding: weakest indicator
link, a hard external dependency (no relay-sec tagged release yet), and
a kill criterion that predicts its own most likely outcome. It returns
when relay-sec tags a release; until then SSC-AUTH/FRESH/CONF stay
honestly marked assumed in the trace.
